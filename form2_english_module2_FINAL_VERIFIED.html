02. Tenses: Present & Past

DSKP 要求复习 present simple / continuous 与 past simple / continuous。

Present Simple

Use: habits, routines, facts. 习惯、日常、事实
Form: I/you/we/they + base verb; he/she/it + verb-s/es.
Wrong: Sara walk to school every day.
Correct: Sara walks to school every day.

Present Continuous

Form: am/is/are + V-ing. 现在正在发生
The boys are playing football now.

Past Simple

Form: V2; negative/question: did + base verb.
Wrong: She did not went.
Correct: She did not go.

Past Continuous

Form: was/were + V-ing. Often used for an action in progress in the past.
I was studying when the phone rang.

06. Question Forms & Indirect Questions

Direct question

Wh-word + auxiliary + subject + verb?
Where does Ali live?

Indirect question

Starter + question word + subject + verb
Do you know where Ali lives?
Wrong: Can you tell me where does he live?
Correct: Can you tell me where he lives?

Indirect question 后面恢复 statement word order。这个位置很适合出 error correction，因为学生会忍不住把 does 搬进去。
